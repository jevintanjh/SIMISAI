## Time Management Tips for Part-Time Founders

### Weekday Evenings (2 hours/night, 3 nights/week):
- **Monday/Tuesday:** Email outreach (associations, AI Singapore)
- **Wednesday/Thursday:** Grant application work (Startup SG Tech, IMDA)
- **Friday:** Customer discovery calls or follow-ups

### Weekends (5-8 hours total):
- **Saturday morning:** Product development (SageMaker fixes, demo video)
- **Saturday afternoon:** Customer discovery (LinkedIn outreach, networking events)
- **Sunday morning:** Content creation (LinkedIn article, case studies)
- **Sunday afternoon:** Week planning and follow-ups

### Total Weekly Commitment:
- **Weekdays:** 6 hours (3 evenings x 2 hours)
- **Weekends:** 5-8 hours
- **Total:** 11-14 hours/week (manageable alongside day job)

**Energy Management:**
- Don't work on SIMISAI every single day (burnout risk)
- Block 1-2 "no-startup" days per week for rest
- Focus on high-impact tasks (customer acquisition > product polish)
- Use templates for repetitive tasks (email outreach, pilot proposals)
