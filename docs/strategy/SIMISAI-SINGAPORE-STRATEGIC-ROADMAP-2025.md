3. **Quality Control & Inspection**:
   - Visual defect detection with AI guidance
   - Step-by-step inspection procedures

- **Traditional LMS platforms** (no computer vision, no real-time AI guidance)
- **AR/VR training** (expensive, not SME-accessible)

**SIMISAI Differentiation**:
- Computer vision device detection (YOLO-based) for hands-free guidance

## 9. Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Risk 1: Customer Acquisition Taking Longer Than Expected
**Mitigation:**
- **Government channels first**: GeBIZ tenders + manufacturing associations provide structured pathways (not cold outreach)
- **Lower pilot pricing**: If struggling to close pilots at S$10,000, drop to S$3,000-5,000 to get case studies
- **Freemium model**: Offer free 1-month trial, then convert to paid (reduces friction)

### Risk 2: Product Development Falling Behind (Limited Engineering Hours)
**Mitigation:**
- **Hire part-time contractors**: Use pilot revenue to hire 1-2 part-time engineers (Singapore has strong freelance dev market)
- **Focus on core features**: Deprioritize nice-to-haves, focus on CV device detection + AI chat guidance only
- **SageMaker fixes**: Allocate weekends for SageMaker model archive fix (current blocker)

### Risk 3: Founder Burnout (Day Job + Startup)
**Mitigation:**
- **80/20 rule**: Focus 80% of startup time on customer acquisition (revenue), 20% on product (only critical fixes)
- **Scheduled startup time**: Block 3 evenings/week + 1 weekend day (don't let it bleed into all free time)
- **Quit jobs earlier if needed**: If burnout risk is high and you have S$50,000 ARR + 6 months personal savings, quit early

### Risk 4: Grant Applications Rejected
**Mitigation:**
- **Reapply**: Startup SG Tech allows reapplication after addressing feedback
- **Alternative grants**: If Startup SG Tech rejected, try social enterprise grants, IMDA grants, AI Singapore research grants
- **Pivot to angel funding**: If all grants fail, focus on revenue traction (S$100K ARR) to attract angels

### Risk 5: Enterprise Sales Slower Than Expected (Part-Time Limitation)
**Mitigation:**
- **Hire part-time sales advisor**: Use pilot revenue to hire ex-manufacturing sales professional (commission-based, 10 hours/week)
- **Automate demos**: Record self-serve product tour video, let customers explore async (reduces live demo time)
- **Pilot-to-annual conversion focus**: Spend 80% of time ensuring pilot success (not chasing new customers)
