# SIMISAI B2B Partnership Research: Singapore & Southeast Asia

**Research Date:** December 13, 2025
**Focus:** Computer Vision + Multilingual AI Chat Platform B2B Opportunities
**Region:** Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia

---

## Executive Summary

SIMISAI's proven YOLOv11 computer vision platform with multilingual AI chat (English, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino) presents strong B2B opportunities across 6 key industries in Southeast Asia. The platform's medical device success provides credibility for safety-critical applications.

**Top 3 Recommended Industries for Immediate Pursuit:**
1. **Logistics/Warehouse** - Fastest ROI, clear pain points, regional expansion happening now
2. **Retail** - High volume transactions, immediate cost savings, strong SEA presence
3. **CCTV/Security** - Government procurement opportunities, long-term contracts, safety-critical credibility

**Market Opportunity:** SEA AI/CV market projected at $12B+ by 2026, growing at 25%+ CAGR
