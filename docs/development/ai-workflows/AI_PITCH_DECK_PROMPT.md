# AI Presentation Prompt: SIMISAI 5-Minute Investor Pitch

**Objective**: Generate a professional, visually engaging 10-slide presentation for a 5-minute investor pitch. The tone should be confident, innovative, and focused on a massive market opportunity with a proven solution.

---

### **Slide 1: Title Slide**
*   **Title**: SIMISAI: Guiding the Future of Connected Devices
*   **Subtitle**: AI-Powered Vision for Healthcare and Beyond
*   **Visual**: A powerful, clean image showing an elderly person's hands holding a smartphone, with the phone's camera successfully identifying a medical device next to it. Include the SIMISAI logo and a badge saying "Winner - AI Singapore Sealion Challenge".

---

### **Slide 2: The Problem**
*   **Title**: A Growing Crisis in Care
*   **Key Point 1**: 80 million elderly in Southeast Asia are being left behind by complex technology, especially in healthcare.
*   **Key Point 2**: Language and literacy barriers with essential medical devices lead to misuse, poor health outcomes, and stress for caregivers.
*   **Visual**: A split-screen visual. On the left, a photo of a confused-looking senior trying to use a blood pressure monitor. On the right, a simple, impactful statistic: "65% of seniors in SEA have low digital literacy."

---

### **Slide 3: Our Solution**
*   **Title**: SIMISAI: Your AI Device Assistant
*   **Key Point 1**: **Point & Identify**: Our Computer Vision instantly recognizes any device.
*   **Key Point 2**: **Understand & Guide**: We provide real-time, step-by-step instructions in 5 local languages.
*   **Key Point 3**: **Support & Empower**: An AI chat answers questions, turning confusion into confidence.
*   **Visual**: A simple 3-panel graphic showing the app flow: (1) Phone scanning a device, (2) App showing clear instructions, (3) App showing a chat interface.

---

### **Slide 4: Why Now? The Market Opportunity**
*   **Title**: A $14.6 Billion Market at a Tipping Point
*   **Key Point 1 (TAM)**: The Southeast Asia home healthcare market is booming, driven by an aging population.
*   **Key Point 2 (SAM)**: Our initial focus is the $395M device monitoring market, where no competitor offers active guidance.
*   **Visual**: A set of concentric circles visualizing TAM, SAM, and our initial target market (SOM). Include key stats like "9.5% CAGR".

---

### **Slide 5: The Technology**
*   **Title**: Proven, Scalable, and Defensible
*   **Key Point 1**: **Core AI**: Built on a robust foundation of YOLOv8 Computer Vision and the Sealion LLM for multilingual support.

*   **Visual**: A clean, high-level architecture diagram. Avoid overly technical jargon. Show icons for CV, LLM, and AWS, indicating a solid tech stack.

*   **Key Point 3 (B2B2C)**: Partnerships with device manufacturers for co-marketing and pre-installation deals.
