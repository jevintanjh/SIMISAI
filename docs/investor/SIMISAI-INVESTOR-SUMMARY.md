## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**SIMISAI** is a universal visual guidance platform that combines computer vision, augmented reality, and multilingual AI to help anyone operate any device or complete any physical task. We won Singapore's national AI competition by solving the hardest guidance problem—teaching elderly users with low digital literacy how to use medical devices—and are now applying that validated technology to a $50B+ commercial market across enterprise training, consumer products, and retail.

## THE OPPORTUNITY

### Market Size:
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $50B+ globally
  - Enterprise training & support: $10B+ (APAC: $2B+)
  - Consumer smart home/IoT: $150B+ (APAC: $30B+)
  - Retail technology: $25B+
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: $5B+ (Southeast Asia + initial enterprise)
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: $250M (Year 3 target)

### Problem:
- **Enterprise**: $8B+ wasted annually on ineffective training, technician dispatch, operational errors
- **Consumer**: 30% product return rate due to setup difficulty, $15B in support costs
- **Retail**: 40% of customers abandon purchases due to lack of product information

### Solution:
SIMISAI provides real-time, personalized visual guidance through:
1. **Computer Vision**: Instantly identifies objects through phone camera (MediaPipe + YOLOv8)
2. **Augmented Reality**: Step-by-step visual instructions overlaid on real-world objects
3. **AI Chat**: Multilingual conversational support (Sealion LLM - 5 Southeast Asian languages)
