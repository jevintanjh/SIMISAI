# AIMX Conference Presentation - Image Sourcing Guide

## Image Requirements Summary

**Technical Specs:**
- Minimum Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Preferred Resolution: 3840x2160 (4K) for projection quality
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File size: 500KB - 5MB (optimized for presentations)

**Content Requirements:**
- Southeast Asian subjects strongly preferred (Filipino, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese)
- Authentic, dignified representation of elderly (not stock "helpless senior" clichés)
- Home healthcare contexts, not clinical/hospital settings (except Slide 2b)
- Natural lighting and genuine emotions

## Licensing and Attribution Guide

### Creative Commons Licenses (Acceptable for Commercial Use)

**CC0 (Public Domain):**
- No attribution required
- Free for commercial use
- Found on: Unsplash, Pixabay, Pexels

**CC BY (Attribution):**
- Attribution required
- Free for commercial use
- Format: "Photo by [Photographer] on [Platform]"

**CC BY-SA (Attribution-ShareAlike):**
- Attribution required
- Free for commercial use
- Derivative works must use same license

### Platform-Specific Licenses

**Unsplash License:**
- Free for commercial use
- No attribution required (but appreciated)
- Cannot create competing image service
- Attribution format: "Photo by [Name] on Unsplash"

**Pexels License:**
- Free for commercial use
- No attribution required
- Cannot sell unmodified photos
- Attribution format: "Photo by [Name] from Pexels"

**Pixabay License:**
- Free for commercial use
- No attribution required
- Cannot redistribute on other stock sites
- Attribution format: "Image by [Name] from Pixabay"

### Attribution Best Practices for Presentation

**On the slide (if required):**
- Small text in bottom corner
- Format: "Photo: [Photographer] / [Platform]"
- Font size: 8-10pt, subtle color (not distracting)

**In appendix slide:**
- Create "Image Credits" slide at end of deck
- List all images with full attribution
- Include URLs for verification

## Image Sourcing Workflow

### Step 1: Search Free Platforms First (1-2 hours)

1. **Unsplash** (Start here - highest quality free images)
   - Use specific search terms from above
   - Filter by "Landscape" orientation for wide shots
   - Download "Large" size (typically 4K+)

2. **Pexels** (Good for diverse subjects)
   - Search with same terms
   - Check "Similar Photos" for variations
   - Download highest resolution available

3. **Pixabay** (Good backup, more stock-like)
   - Search with keywords
   - Filter by "Horizontal" orientation
   - Download "Original" size

### Step 2: Evaluate Southeast Asian Stock Platforms (If budget allows)

**Asian-Focused Stock Sites:**
- **123RF Philippines** - Good Filipino subjects
- **Dreamstime Asia** - Southeast Asian collection
- **Depositphotos Asia** - Thai and Indonesian subjects

**Cost:** $10-50 per image (single use license)

### Step 3: Check Medical Device Manufacturer Resources

**Free promotional images from:**
- Omron Healthcare Asia (blood pressure monitors)
- Philips Healthcare APAC (home healthcare)
- iHealth Labs (mobile health devices)

**Usage:** Often allow use with attribution for non-competing purposes

### Step 4: NGO and Government Health Resources

**Creative Commons Sources:**
- WHO Southeast Asia Regional Office photo library
- USAID Asia healthcare programs
- ADB (Asian Development Bank) healthcare initiatives
- Local health ministries (Philippines DOH, Indonesia Ministry of Health)

**Search:**
- Look for press releases and campaign materials
- Check Flickr accounts with CC licenses
- Contact communications offices for permission

### Step 5: Paid Stock as Final Option (If needed)

**Premium Platforms:**
- **Shutterstock** - Excellent search, high quality ($29-49/image)
- **Getty Images** - Editorial photos, authentic subjects ($125-500/image)
- **iStock** - Mid-range quality and price ($15-30/image)

**Budget Consideration:**
- Total for 6 images: $90-300 (Shutterstock/iStock range)
- Conference usage license typically included
- May need extended license for large venue (>5000 people)

## Image Quality Checklist

Before finalizing each image, verify:

**Technical Quality:**
- [ ] Resolution 1920x1080 minimum (preferably 3840x2160)
- [ ] Sharp focus on subject (not motion blur or soft focus)
- [ ] Good exposure (not underexposed or blown out highlights)
- [ ] Color accurate (not heavy filters or unrealistic tones)
- [ ] File size 500KB - 5MB (large enough for quality, not unwieldy)

**Content Quality:**
- [ ] Subject ethnicity appropriate (Asian, preferably Southeast Asian)
- [ ] Age appropriate (elderly = 60+ years, not 50s)
- [ ] Dignified representation (not stereotypical or demeaning)
- [ ] Authentic emotion (genuine smiles, natural expressions)
- [ ] Relevant context (home healthcare, not hospital unless specified)

**Licensing Quality:**
- [ ] License permits commercial use (conferences count as commercial)
- [ ] License permits use in presentations
- [ ] Attribution requirements noted and can be met
- [ ] Source URL saved for future verification
- [ ] Photographer/creator information recorded

**Presentation Quality:**
- [ ] Composition allows text overlay space (Slide 1 especially)
- [ ] Background not too busy or distracting
- [ ] Lighting consistent across image set (warm, natural tone)
- [ ] Cultural appropriateness verified
- [ ] Emotional tone matches slide purpose

## Emergency Fallback Plan

**If no suitable Southeast Asian images found within 3 hours:**

1. **Use broader "Asian elderly" search** (Chinese, Japanese, Korean subjects)
   - Still culturally closer than Western subjects
   - Acceptable for technical conference context

2. **Commission custom photography** (If budget and timeline allow)
   - Local photographers in Manila, Jakarta, Bangkok
   - Cost: $200-500 for half-day shoot
   - Timeline: 1-2 weeks for scheduling + editing

3. **Use illustration instead of photos** (Last resort)
   - Tasteful medical device illustrations
   - Icon-based slides
   - Maintains professional appearance without cultural mismatch
