## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS

### Issue: "Can't find elderly Asian subjects"
**Solutions**:
1. **Broaden to "Asian"** instead of "Southeast Asian" (Chinese, Japanese, Korean acceptable)
2. **Search by country**: "Filipino elderly", "Thai grandmother", "Indonesian senior"
3. **Search by city**: "elderly woman Bangkok", "senior Jakarta"
4. **Use Pexels over Unsplash** (Pexels has more diverse subject representation)

### Issue: "All images look too 'stock photo' staged"
**Solutions**:
1. Add "authentic" or "candid" to search terms
2. Look for images with natural lighting (not studio setups)
3. Prefer outdoor or home settings over studio backgrounds
4. Look for photographer names like "Ketut Subiyanto", "Andrea Piacquadio", "Yan Krukau" on Pexels (known for authentic portraits)

### Issue: "Medical devices not visible in images"
**Solutions**:
1. **Component search**: Find elderly portrait separately + blood pressure monitor separately, combine on slide
2. Search "hands blood pressure monitor" (shows device without needing face)
3. Acceptable compromise: Elderly person in healthcare context (even without visible device)

### Issue: "Can't find multi-generational family"
**Solutions**:
1. **Two generations is acceptable**: Grandparent + grandchild works
2. Search broader: "asian family together" or "asian family celebration"
3. Activity-based: "asian family cooking", "asian family reading"
4. **Single elderly + caring context**: If truly limited, single confident elderly person in warm setting is acceptable for closing slide
