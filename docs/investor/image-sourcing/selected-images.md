## 💡 Best Practices Going Forward

### **Image Selection Checklist**
Before selecting any image, verify:
- [ ] Source is Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, or Wikimedia Commons
- [ ] License explicitly allows commercial use
- [ ] Image is minimum 1920x1080 resolution
- [ ] Not a Google Images thumbnail or redirect
- [ ] Not from a competitor's website
- [ ] Not from a news publication (unless CC-licensed)

### **Red Flags to Avoid**
❌ Google Images search results (not a source)
❌ Encrypted Google thumbnails
❌ Competitor websites
❌ News publication images
❌ Social media images
❌ Images without clear licensing
❌ Low-resolution thumbnails

### **Trusted Sources Only**
✅ Unsplash (unsplash.com)
✅ Pexels (pexels.com)
✅ Pixabay (pixabay.com)
✅ Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) - check individual licenses
✅ Custom photography (original content)
