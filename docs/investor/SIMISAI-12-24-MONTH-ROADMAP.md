# SIMISAI 12-24 Month Milestone Roadmap
## Strategic Growth Plan: Enterprise First, Medical Later

**Prepared**: November 2025
**Strategic Focus**: Commercial revenue (enterprise/consumer) before medical regulation
**Target**: $3M-$4M ARR by Month 24, Series A readiness

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This roadmap prioritizes **fast commercial revenue** through enterprise and consumer applications while positioning medical as a **long-term expansion opportunity**. The key strategic insight: **You don't need regulatory approval to generate revenue and prove your technology works.**

### Key Assumptions:
- **Seed funding**: $800K-$1.2M (equity) + $400K-$800K (government grants) = $1.2M-$2M total capital
- **Burn rate**: $60K-$80K/month (Months 0-12), $80K-$120K/month (Months 12-24)
- **Focus markets**: Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam
- **Target verticals**: Enterprise (manufacturing, logistics, field services), Consumer (electronics, furniture, smart home), Retail (in-store, e-commerce)

## KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

### Critical Path Items (Must-Haves):

1. **First enterprise pilot success** (Month 6): Proves ROI, creates case study for sales
2. **First white-label partnership** (Month 9): Validates consumer model, shows platform scalability
3. **$100K MRR** (Month 12): Milestone for Series A credibility
4. **Geographic expansion proof** (Month 12-18): Shows multi-market viability
5. **Product-market fit validation** (Month 18): NRR >110%, <5% churn demonstrates retention

---

### Risk Mitigation Strategies:

**Risk**: Enterprise sales cycles longer than expected (6-12 months vs. 3-6 months)
- **Mitigation**: Focus on smaller companies (50-200 employees) with faster decision-making
- **Backup plan**: Increase pilot volume (5-7 vs. 3-5) to maintain conversion pipeline

**Risk**: White-label partnerships slow to close (9-18 months negotiation)
- **Mitigation**: Launch B2C consumer app (freemium model) as backup revenue stream
- **Backup plan**: Focus on smaller regional brands with faster decision cycles

**Risk**: Geographic expansion challenges (regulatory, cultural, operational)
- **Mitigation**: Double down on Singapore/Thailand before Vietnam/Indonesia
- **Backup plan**: Partner with local system integrators instead of direct expansion

**Risk**: Medical regulatory delays (12-24 months vs. 6-9 months)
- **Mitigation**: Delay medical launch, focus on commercial verticals (already profitable without medical)
- **Backup plan**: Medical becomes Phase 4 (Month 24+) after Series A, not Phase 3

**Risk**: Technology scaling issues (accuracy drops, performance degrades)
- **Mitigation**: Invest 40% of seed funding in engineering to build robust infrastructure
- **Backup plan**: Limit device model expansion, focus on quality over quantity

**Risk**: Competition emerges (AR platforms add AI, enterprise software adds CV)
- **Mitigation**: Build data moat through rapid device model expansion (100+ models by Month 18)
- **Backup plan**: Pursue white-label partnerships with exclusive contracts
