## 🎯 RECOMMENDED PREPARATION WORKFLOW

### **Week 1: Content Preparation**
1. Read `SIMISAI-5MIN-PITCH-DECK.md` (familiarize with slides)
2. Convert pitch deck to PowerPoint/Keynote (add visuals)
3. Practice full pitch 3-5 times (with timer)
4. Review `SIMISAI-15MIN-PRESENTATION-WORKFLOW.md` (understand full process)

### **Week 1-2: Demo Preparation**
1. Read `SIMISAI-DEMO-SCRIPT.md` (understand demo flow)
2. Test live demo 5+ times (on venue Wi-Fi if possible)
3. Record backup demo video (3-4 minutes, high quality)
4. Prepare 2 phones + backup video on laptop

### **1 Day Before: Final Prep**
1. Follow checklist in `SIMISAI-15MIN-PRESENTATION-WORKFLOW.md` (1 Day Before section)
2. Full dress rehearsal with timer (target: 10 minutes for pitch + demo)
3. Print all materials (workflow, demo script, one-pagers)
4. Charge all devices to 100%

### **Day Of: Execution**
1. Follow "Morning Of" and "On-Site" checklists in workflow document
2. Arrive 30 minutes early (14:25 for 14:55 slot)
3. Test venue Wi-Fi, projector, and demo
4. Keep demo script in pocket (quick reference)
5. Deep breath, smile, and deliver!

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### **Before Presentation**:
✅ Sleep well the night before (8+ hours)
✅ Arrive 30 minutes early (test everything)
✅ Practice deep breathing (calm nerves)
✅ Visualize success (positive mindset)
✅ Review opening hook and closing statement (memorize these!)

### **During Presentation**:
✅ Make eye contact with all judges (rotate attention)
✅ Smile and show enthusiasm (investors invest in people)
✅ Speak clearly and with energy (not too fast)
✅ Handle failures gracefully (backup plans exist)
✅ Stay within time limit (respect judges' schedule)

### **During Q&A**:
✅ Listen to full question before answering (don't interrupt)
✅ Pause 1-2 seconds before responding (shows thoughtfulness)
✅ Answer concisely (30-45 seconds max per question)
✅ If you don't know, say: "Great question—I'll follow up with data"
✅ Stay calm and confident (even with tough questions)

### **After Presentation**:
✅ Follow up within 24 hours (strike while iron is hot)
✅ Personalize each follow-up email (reference their questions)
✅ Offer next steps (calendar link, additional materials)
✅ Track investor responses in CRM
✅ Celebrate! (you earned it!)
