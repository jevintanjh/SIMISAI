### 3. Create Process Flow Diagrams (Slide 3)
**Impact**: 🔴 CRITICAL - "How SIMISAI works" must be scannable in 5 seconds

**What to Do**:


**Research Backing**: Successful healthtech decks use "simplified workflow diagrams—before-and-after showing 'before' cluttered, 'after' clean"

## CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY GUIDELINES (Critical for Southeast Asia Focus)

### What "Respectful Elderly Representation" Looks Like

**Good Example**:
- Elderly person actively doing something (using device, confident)
- Expression shows capability, wisdom
- Family present as equals, not "helpers"
- Modern home setting
- Technology visible but not overwhelming

**Bad Example**:
- Elderly person passive, being "helped"
- Expression shows confusion, frailty, dependence
- Clinical/hospital setting (medicalizes aging)
- No technology visible
- Traditional clothing as cultural signifier (tokenistic)

### Regional Considerations
- **Indonesia**: Family/community context valued, religious respect
- **Philippines**: Family bonds central, resilience and positivity
- **Thailand**: Respect for elders as wisdom holders, dignity paramount
- **Vietnam**: Filial piety deeply valued, heritage respected
- **Singapore**: Technological advancement celebrated, multiethnic representation

**Key Principle**: Show elderly as **confident, capable, respected family members** using technology successfully—NOT as dependent or needing "rescue"
