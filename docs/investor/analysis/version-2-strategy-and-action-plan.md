### C. Cultural Considerations for Southeast Asian Imagery

#### Visual Representation Best Practices by Market

**Shared Southeast Asian Cultural Values**:
1. **Respect for Elders**: Show elderly as wise, capable, respected (not dependent)
2. **Family-Centric**: Multi-generational context preferred over isolated elderly
3. **Dignity and Modesty**: Avoid medical vulnerability or exposing frailty
4. **Technological Capability**: Show successful technology adoption (counters "elderly can't use tech" stereotype)
5. **Community Context**: Home/family settings > clinical/hospital settings

**Specific Cultural Nuances by Country**:

| Country | Key Visual Themes | Color Preferences | Settings to Emphasize | Avoid |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------|-------|
| **Singapore** | Modern, tech-forward, multi-cultural | Cool blues, professional teal | Clean modern homes, tech integration | Overly traditional imagery |
| **Indonesia** | Family, community, religious respect | Warm earth tones, greens | Multi-generational homes, community | Clinical sterility, isolation |
| **Philippines** | Family bonds, faith, resilience | Vibrant colors, warm tones | Family gatherings, home care | Patronizing "charity" framing |
| **Thailand** | Respect, tradition, harmony | Golds, oranges (royal/spiritual) | Traditional homes, Buddhist context | Stereotypical "exotic" imagery |
| **Vietnam** | Heritage, filial piety, progress | Red (luck), gold (prosperity) | Urban + rural balance, family meals | War/poverty imagery (outdated) |

**Medical Device Imagery in Asian Contexts**:
1. **Normalize Technology**: Show medical devices as everyday tools, not intimidating medical equipment
2. **Home Health Emphasis**: Living rooms, kitchens, familiar spaces (not hospitals)
3. **Color Sensitivity**: White = death in some cultures (use warm, inviting colors)
4. **Avoid Clinical Sterility**: Warm lighting, natural settings, family presence
5. **Empowerment, Not Dependence**: Show users independently successful, not being "helped"

**Avoiding Tokenism**:
- ❌ **DON'T**: Use one generic "Asian" person to represent all of Southeast Asia
- ✅ **DO**: Show diversity across countries (Filipino grandmother ≠ Thai grandmother)
- ❌ **DON'T**: Use traditional clothing as cultural "proof" (can feel stereotypical)
- ✅ **DO**: Show authentic modern daily life in each country
- ❌ **DON'T**: Frame elderly as "problems to solve" (paternalistic)
- ✅ **DO**: Frame elderly as "users we empower" (respectful, capability-focused)

### Design Directive Additions for Visual-First Approach

**Add to Gamma.app Prompt - Global Section**:

```markdown
## VISUAL-FIRST MANDATE (CRITICAL)

Every slide must pass the "10-Foot Test":
- Can an investor read the text from 10 feet away? (Font ≥18pt)
- Can an investor understand the KEY MESSAGE in 5 seconds WITHOUT reading text?
- Does the visual dominate (60%+) or does text dominate?

**Slide Review Questions** (Gamma should optimize for "YES" to all):
1. Could I turn off the projector sound and investor would still understand the slide? ✅
2. Does the largest element on the slide SHOW (not tell) the key message? ✅
3. Are there ≤3 text blocks total on the slide? ✅
4. Is whitespace at least 30% of slide area? ✅
5. Could I replace any remaining text with an icon or image? ✅

**Text Reduction Formula**:
- If slide has >50 words → CUT 60%, convert to visuals
- If bullet points present → REPLACE with icons + 2-3 word labels
- If statistics in sentences → EXTRACT to infographic/chart
- If explanatory paragraphs → DELETE (speaker will provide context)

**Visual Hierarchy Enforcement**:
1. **Hero Element** (largest, first-seen): Must be visual (photo, chart, diagram)—NEVER text
2. **Supporting Elements** (medium, second-seen): Icons, small charts, logos
3. **Text** (smallest, last-seen): Titles, key numbers, ultra-minimal body text
```

## PITFALLS TO AVOID (Common Mistakes in Version 1)

Based on critique document analysis, actively avoid these patterns:

### 1. Text Walls (Version 1 Problem)
❌ **DON'T**: Write paragraph-length explanations on slides
✅ **DO**: Use headline (5-8 words) + supporting visual + minimal body text (10-15 words)

### 2. Generic Stock Photos (Authenticity Issue)
❌ **DON'T**: Use Western-centric or AI-generated imagery
✅ **DO**: Source authentic Southeast Asian subjects from Pexels with cultural appropriateness

### 3. Statistics as Text (Boring, Forgettable)
❌ **DON'T**: Write "76 million elderly in Southeast Asia struggle with medical devices"
✅ **DO**: Show 76M as visual grid of person icons with large "76M" number + map

### 4. Feature Dump (Product Slide)
❌ **DON'T**: List technology features (MediaPipe, YOLOv8, Sealion LLM) as bullet points
✅ **DO**: Show simple 4-step process flow with icons: Camera → AI → Guidance → Success

### 5. Weak Competitive Differentiation (Lost Opportunity)
❌ **DON'T**: Write paragraphs explaining "why we're better than competitors"
✅ **DO**: Show color-coded comparison grid with ✓ (green) for SIMISAI, ✗ (red) for competitors

### 6. No Visual Hierarchy (Cognitive Overload)
❌ **DON'T**: Fill every inch of slide with content (text + images competing for attention)
✅ **DO**: Apply 60-30-10 rule (60% visual, 30% whitespace, 10% text)—let slides breathe

❌ **DON'T**: Mention AI Singapore win only once in tiny text
✅ **DO**: AI Singapore badge/logo on 40% of slides (1, 4, 7, 10)—makes it unforgettable
