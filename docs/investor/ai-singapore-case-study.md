# **SIMIS AI - Developer Challenge Case Study**
## Healthcare Track Winner - Pan-SEA AI Developer Challenge 2025

---

## **Overview**

In Southeast Asia's rural and underserved communities, elderly residents struggle to use medical devices correctly—leading to preventable health complications and hospital readmissions. This challenge is amplified by low digital literacy, multiple languages, and complex device interfaces that weren't designed for aging populations.

**SIMIS AI** addresses this critical healthcare gap using computer vision and multilingual AI to provide real-time, step-by-step guidance for medical devices like blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, and thermometers. By winning the Healthcare Track of AI Singapore's Pan-SEA AI Developer Challenge, we've demonstrated a promising approach to bridge the accessibility gap for Southeast Asia's 680 million people—starting with those who need it most.

---

## **Solution**

SIMIS AI's proof-of-concept demonstrates three breakthrough technologies for accessible medical device guidance:

### **1. Computer Vision Device Detection**
- Uses MediaPipe and a custom-trained **YOLOv11** model to recognize medical devices through smartphone camera
- Demo supports 3 device types: blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, and thermometers
- Designed for real-time detection without requiring specialized hardware

### **2. Multilingual AI Chat Assistant**
- Leverages Singapore's Sealion LLM—purpose-built for Southeast Asian languages
- Provides real-time conversational guidance in 5 languages: English, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Filipino
- Adapts instructions based on user responses and device type detected

### **3. Scalable Cloud Architecture**
- Built on AWS serverless infrastructure for easy scaling
- PostgreSQL database for device-specific guidance and multilingual instruction sets
- Real-time WebSocket communication for instant AI responses

### **What Makes Our Approach Unique**

**Safety-Critical Focus**: We're tackling the hardest use case first—elderly users with medical devices—where accessibility and accuracy matter most.

**Southeast Asia-First Design**: Built with Sealion LLM for authentic regional language support, not Western-centric translations. Our multilingual approach respects cultural nuances in medical terminology across Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines.

**Healthcare-AI Integration**: Our founding team's pharmacy background ensures medical accuracy and patient safety are embedded in every design decision, from device detection algorithms to conversational AI responses.

**Competition-Validated**: Winning AI Singapore's Healthcare Track provides credibility for healthcare partnerships, potential regulatory pathways across ASEAN, and enterprise pilot opportunities.
