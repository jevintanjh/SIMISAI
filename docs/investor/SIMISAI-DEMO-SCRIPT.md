## 🎬 DEMO TRANSITION (15 seconds)

**Say**:
> "Now, let me show you SIMISAI in action. This is our live platform—the same technology that won the AI Singapore competition—working in real-time right now."

**Do**:
1. ✅ Minimize/pause pitch deck
2. ✅ Pull out phone (unlocked, demo URL loaded)
3. ✅ Hold up blood pressure monitor to audience
4. ✅ Ensure good lighting

---

## 📱 STEP 1: Device Detection (60 seconds)

### **Say**:
> "Watch this. I'm pointing my phone camera at this blood pressure monitor. SIMISAI's computer vision—powered by YOLOv8 and MediaPipe—instantly recognizes the device."

### **Do**:
- Hold device steady 12 inches from camera
- Wait for green detection box (2-3 seconds)
- Point out the detection box to audience

### **Say** (when detected) - VALUE BOMB:
> "Two seconds. That's how long SIMISAI takes to identify any device. Compare that to an employee flipping through a 200-page manual for 10 minutes—or calling support and waiting on hold for 15. **This just saved your employee 14 minutes and 58 seconds. Multiply that by 1,000 employees asking 5 questions a week, and you've just recovered 1,300 employee hours per month.** That's $65,000 in labor costs if you're paying $50 an hour. **From device detection alone.**"

**Sabri Suby Technique**: Quantify ROI immediately (time saved → labor costs saved → specific dollar amount)

### **If detection fails**:
- Move device slightly
- Adjust lighting/angle
- If still failing after 5 seconds → "Sometimes lighting affects it—let me adjust" → try once more
- If fails again → go to BACKUP PLAN B

---

## 📋 STEP 2: Guidance Interface (60 seconds)

### **Say**:
> "Now SIMISAI shows me step-by-step instructions in real-time. Look—here's how to position the cuff, where to place it on my arm, when to press start. All in my language—we support English, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Filipino."

### **Do**:
- Click on detected device
- Wait for guidance screen to load (1-2 seconds)
- Scroll through 2-3 instruction steps (slowly but confidently)
- Tilt screen toward judges if needed

### **Say** (while scrolling) - VALUE BOMB:
> "See these AR overlays? **This is what eliminated that $52,000 in downtime at the Singapore factory I mentioned earlier.** The operator would have guessed. He would have broken something. He would have cost the company a day of lost production. Instead, he followed these overlays and got it right the first time. **That's the difference between a $52,000 mistake and a $0 mistake.** And it's not just factories—this works for elderly users with medical devices, consumers assembling IKEA furniture, retail customers setting up smart home products. **One platform. Every guidance problem.**"

**Sabri Suby Technique**: Connect feature directly to the pain story told in Slide 2 (callback to $52K factory shutdown) + show versatility

### **If screen doesn't load**:
- Wait 2 more seconds
- If still blank → "Backend is loading" → wait 2 more seconds
- If fails → go to BACKUP PLAN B

---

## 💬 STEP 3: AI Chat (60 seconds)

### **Say**:
> "Now here's the magic. If users have questions, they can ask our AI assistant—powered by Singapore's Sealion LLM—in their native language."

### **Do**:
- Open AI chat interface
- Type or speak: **"What if the reading is too high?"**
- Wait for AI response (3-5 seconds)
- Show response to audience

### **Say** (when response appears) - VALUE BOMB:
> "Three seconds. That's how long it took to answer a question. Now here's the value bomb: **Every question answered by this AI chat? That's a support call you're NOT paying $35 for.** If you have 1,000 employees asking 5 questions a month, that's 5,000 support calls. At $35 per call, you're spending **$175,000 annually on call center costs.** This AI assistant just eliminated that entire expense. **And it answered in 3 seconds instead of a 15-minute hold time.** That's computer vision for detection, augmented reality for guidance, and AI chat for questions—all in one platform. All in their native language. All eliminating costs you're paying right now."

**Sabri Suby Technique**: Stack the value (ROI calculation) + time comparison + reinforce "all in one platform" positioning

### **If AI fails to respond**:
- Wait 3 more seconds
- If still loading → "Network latency—let me show you a cached response" → scroll to previous chat
- If completely broken → skip to Step 4 and acknowledge gracefully

## 🎯 STEP 4: Demo Closing (30 seconds)

### **Say** - VALUE BOMB FINALE:
> "That's SIMISAI—production-ready, deployed on AWS, and working right now. **This isn't a prototype. This is live, in production, scaling today.** You could deploy this in your factory next Monday. By Friday, you'd start seeing training time cut in half. By the end of the month, you'd have your first prevented equipment failure. **That's the difference between a concept and a solution.** The same technology scales across enterprise training, consumer products, and retail—because if it works for an 80-year-old grandmother with a blood pressure monitor, **it works for anyone, anywhere, with anything.**"

**Sabri Suby Technique**: Concrete timeline (Monday → Friday → End of month) + confidence close ("concept vs. solution") + callback to opening hook (80-year-old grandmother)

### **Do**:
- Put phone down confidently
- Make eye contact with all judges
- Smile (show confidence)
- Pause for 2 seconds (let the impact land)
- Return to pitch deck or closing slide

## 🚨 BACKUP PLANS (MEMORIZE THESE!)

### **BACKUP PLAN A: Internet is Slow**
**Trigger**: Demo loads but slowly (5+ seconds)

**Say**:
> "Network is a bit slow here—actually a great proof point that SIMISAI works on any connection, even with latency."

**Do**: Continue demo, narrate through loading times

---

### **BACKUP PLAN B: Internet Fails Completely**
**Trigger**: Demo won't load, API errors, complete failure

**Say** (calmly, with smile):
> "Ah, the classic live demo challenge! Let me show you our backup video—this is SIMISAI running yesterday with the exact same workflow."

**Do**:
1. Switch to laptop
2. Open backup video file (should already be ready)
3. Play video (2-3 minutes)
4. Narrate over video:
   - "Here you see the device detection..."
   - "Now the step-by-step guidance loads..."
   - "And here's the AI chat answering a question..."
5. End confidently: "This is what I wanted to show you live, and it's running in production right now—just not cooperating for demo purposes today!"

**Key**: Don't panic, don't apologize excessively, maintain confidence

---

### **BACKUP PLAN C: Phone Crashes/Camera Fails**
**Trigger**: Phone freezes, camera permission denied, app crashes

**Say**:
> "Let me grab my backup device—we always prepare for Murphy's Law in live demos."

**Do**:
1. Pull out backup phone (from pocket, should be ready)
2. Open demo URL (should already be loaded)
3. Continue demo from Step 1
4. If backup phone also fails → activate Plan B (video)

---

### **BACKUP PLAN D: Medical Device Not Detected**
**Trigger**: CV model fails to recognize device after multiple attempts

**Say**:
> "This particular device is giving us trouble—let me switch to our thermometer, which is a simpler detection case."

**Do**:
1. Switch to backup medical device (thermometer)
2. Try detection again
3. If still fails → activate Plan B (video)

## 💡 DEMO BEST PRACTICES

### Do's:
✅ **Speak while demonstrating** (don't go silent)
✅ **Hold phone steady** (shaky camera = poor detection)
✅ **Angle screen toward judges** (make sure they can see)
✅ **Move deliberately** (fast movements confuse audience)
✅ **Smile and maintain eye contact** (show confidence)
✅ **Acknowledge issues gracefully** ("Live demos, right?")
✅ **Keep narrating even during failures** (maintain control)

### Don'ts:
❌ **Don't apologize excessively** (1 acknowledgment is enough)
❌ **Don't panic** (backup plans exist)
❌ **Don't go silent during loading** (narrate through delays)
❌ **Don't spend more than 4 minutes total** (demo is not the whole pitch)
❌ **Don't troubleshoot live** (if it fails twice, go to backup)
❌ **Don't blame technology** ("This always works!" = defensive)
❌ **Don't rush through demo** (judges need to see it clearly)

## 📞 EMERGENCY SUPPORT

**If catastrophic failure**:
1. Smile and acknowledge: "Well, Murphy's Law in action!"
2. Switch to backup video immediately (don't waste time troubleshooting)
3. Narrate confidently over video
4. End with: "This is running in production—just not cooperating today!"
5. Move to closing statement (don't dwell on failure)

**Remember**: Investors care more about your reaction to failure than the failure itself. Stay calm, show problem-solving, move on.
