## 📊 Key Statistics (2025 Singapore Logistics)

### The Crisis
- **15-20% labor shortage** (vs 5% national average)
- **35-40% e-commerce growth** (450M → 650M parcels/year)
- **3-5% net margins** (razor-thin profitability)
- **80% can't afford** traditional automation (S$2.5M-4.2M)
- **S$150-300M annual losses** from misrouted parcels

### Workforce Demographics
- **60% foreign workers** (Indonesian, Thai, Filipino, Vietnamese)
- **5+ languages** spoken daily
- **2-4 weeks** training time (language barriers)
- **30-50% annual turnover**

### Current Pain Points
- **3-8% sorting error rate** (manual process)
- **5-10 seconds per package** (manual barcode scan + inspection)
- **5-8% barcode scan failures** (torn labels, angle issues)
- **10-15% damage miss rate** (human fatigue)

## 📅 Pilot Structure (Quick Reference)

### 30-45 Day SME Pilot
- **Week 1**: Setup (install 2-5 cameras, train AI, worker orientation)
- **Week 2-4**: Live deployment (monitored by SIMISAI team)
- **Week 5-6**: Results analysis, ROI calculation, decision meeting

### Success Criteria
- ✅ **>85% detection accuracy**
- ✅ **>30% error reduction**
- ✅ **<8% false positive rate**
- ✅ **>90% system uptime**
- ✅ **Workers rate 3.5/5+** (ease of use)

### Deliverables
- Custom-trained YOLOv11 model
- Weekly progress reports
- Final pilot report (10-15 pages)
- ROI calculation spreadsheet
- Video testimonial (if successful)
