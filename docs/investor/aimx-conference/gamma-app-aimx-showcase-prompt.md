# SIMISAI AIMX Showcase Presentation - Gamma.app Bulk Prompt

**Purpose**: Complete prompt for generating AIMX conference showcase presentation in Gamma.app
**Context**: Healthcare Track Winner showcase for mixed audience (technical, business, investors, general)
**Tone**: Educational, inspiring, collaborative (not aggressive sales)
**Target**: 60% visual, 30% whitespace, 10% text | Max 40-50 words per slide

---

## GLOBAL DESIGN DIRECTIVES

**Apply to ALL slides:**
- Maximum 40-50 words per slide (strictly enforced)
- 60% visual content, 30% whitespace, 10% text
- Use Unsplash high-resolution photos (minimum 1920x1080) - NO AI-generated images
- Color palette: Teal (#00A896), Navy Blue (#002147), Orange (#FF6B35)
- Typography: Sans-serif (Montserrat/Inter), titles 36-48pt, body 18-24pt
- Icons: Consistent modern line-style icons throughout
- Cultural focus: Southeast Asian subjects strongly preferred
- Tone: Educational and inspiring (NOT sales-heavy or aggressive)

## 🎨 ADDITIONAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR GAMMA.APP

### Image Selection Priority:

1. **Always use AUTHENTIC PHOTOGRAPHS** from Unsplash/Pexels (NO AI-generated images)
2. **Prioritize Southeast Asian subjects** (Filipino, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese features)
3. If SE Asian limited, use East Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) as close alternative
4. **Minimum resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
5. **Natural lighting**, authentic emotions, professional quality
6. **Cultural appropriateness**: Show elderly as confident and capable (NOT frail or dependent)

### Visual Design Rules:

1. Every slide must have **60%+ visual content** (images, charts, diagrams, icons)
2. Text limited to **40-50 words maximum** per slide
3. Use **COLOR-CODED elements** (green for positive/SIMISAI, red for negative/problems)
4. Charts must have **LARGE labels**, minimal gridlines
5. Icons must be **consistent style** throughout (modern line icons, 48-64px)
6. **Whitespace is essential** (30% minimum per slide - don't cram content)

### Layout Philosophy:

1. **Visual elements should DOMINATE** the slide (60% minimum)
2. Text should **support visuals**, not compete with them
3. Most important visual should be **largest element** on slide
4. **Avoid text-heavy bullet lists** - use icons and infographics instead
5. **F-pattern reading flow**: Top-left (most important) → center → bottom-right (CTA)

### Color Consistency:

- **Primary: Teal/Turquoise** (#00A896) - SIMISAI brand elements, positive indicators, primary data
- **Secondary: Navy Blue** (#002147) - text, supporting elements, authority
- **Accent: Warm Orange** (#FF6B35) - CTAs, growth indicators, energy elements
- **Success: Green** (#00C851) - checkmarks, positive comparisons, success metrics
- **Urgency: Red** (#CC0000) - use sparingly for problem statements, negative comparisons only

### Typography Standards:

- **All text must be readable from 10 feet** away (minimum 18pt body text)
- **Titles**: Bold sans-serif, 36-48pt
- **Body**: Regular sans-serif, 18-24pt
- **Labels**: 16-18pt minimum
- **Consistent font family** throughout (Montserrat, Inter, or Poppins preferred)
- **High contrast**: 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG AA standard)

### Conference-Specific Adjustments:

1. **Tone is educational/inspiring** (NOT sales-heavy or aggressive)
2. **Healthcare Track Winner** must be prominent (Slides 1, 4, 7, 10, 11 = 45% visibility)
3. **Business opportunity embedded naturally** (not leading with it)
4. **Multiple engagement pathways** on Slide 11 (not just funding ask)
5. **Collaborative language**: "Join us", "Partner with us", "Learn more" (not "Invest now")
