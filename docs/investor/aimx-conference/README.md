## 🎯 Conference vs Investor Pitch - Key Differences

### **Investor Pitch Focus**
- Problem agitation → solution → business model
- Financial metrics and ROI
- Funding ask and use of funds
- Target: Secure investment

### **Conference Showcase Focus**
- Technology demonstration and innovation
- Real-world impact and social good
- AI Singapore competition win validation
- Target: Build awareness, partnerships, pilot customers

## 💡 Conference Presentation Tips

### **Emphasize:**
✅ AI Singapore Sealion Challenge **win** (credibility)
✅ Technology innovation (CV + AI + multilingual)
✅ Social impact (elderly care, accessibility)
✅ Real-world validation (production MVP deployed)

### **De-emphasize:**
❌ Detailed financial projections
❌ Hard funding ask (mention available, but don't push)
❌ Competitor comparisons (focus on your strengths)

### **Conference-Specific Additions:**
- Mention partnership opportunities
- Offer pilot program for interested organizations
- Highlight research collaboration potential
- Emphasize Southeast Asian market expertise

## 🎤 Conference-Adapted Key Messages

**Opening Hook:**
> "We won Singapore's national AI competition by solving one of the hardest guidance problems in AI: teaching elderly Southeast Asians to use medical devices safely. Today, we're here to show you how this technology can transform any physical task guidance."

**Value Proposition:**
> "SIMISAI combines computer vision, conversational AI, and multilingual support to make any physical task accessible to anyone, anywhere—starting with the users who need it most."

**Closing (Conference Version):**
> "We proved our technology works by winning Singapore's national AI competition. Now we're unlocking universal visual guidance for Southeast Asia and beyond. If you're interested in partnerships, pilots, or collaboration, let's connect."
