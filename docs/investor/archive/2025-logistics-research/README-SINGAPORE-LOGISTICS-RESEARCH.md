## Statistics Confidence Guide

**Use with High Confidence (90%+ accurate):**
- Government grant rates (verified on official websites)
- Foreign worker levies (from MOM)
- E-commerce growth trends (e-Conomy SEA reports)
- Traditional automation cost ranges (vendor pricing)

**Use with Context (70-90% accurate):**
- Labor shortage percentages (industry surveys, may vary by company)
- Turnover rates (logistics industry averages)
- Company investment figures (from press releases, may be approximate)
- Cost per parcel breakdowns (synthesized from multiple sources)

**Qualify as "Estimated" (50-70% accurate):**
- Exact parcel volumes (calculated from platform growth rates)
- Industry-wide error costs (extrapolated from sample data)
- AI/CV adoption percentages (limited public disclosure)
- Multilingual training cost breakdowns (industry interviews, not official)

**For Pitch Deck**:
- Lead with high-confidence stats as headline claims
- Use medium-confidence stats as supporting evidence
- Add qualifiers ("estimated", "approximately") for lower-confidence figures
- Keep detailed sources in appendix for due diligence
