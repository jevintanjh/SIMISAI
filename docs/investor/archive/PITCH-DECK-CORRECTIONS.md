## KEY TALKING POINTS

### What the Competition Was
**Pan-SEA AI Developer Challenge 2025**
- Organized by AI Singapore (Singapore Government's National Research Foundation initiative)
- "Pan-SEA" = Regional competition covering Southeast Asia
- "SEA Lion" = AI Singapore's open-source LLM (Southeast Asian Languages in One Network)
- Four tracks: Healthcare, Education, Finance, Public Sector
- Healthcare track problem: "Reimagining Healthcare Access and Intelligence in Southeast Asia using LLM-related solutions"

### What Winning the Healthcare Track Means
1. **Government Validation**: Singapore's National Research Foundation independently verified our technology
2. **Technical Credibility**: Evaluated against competitors in transparent, competitive environment
3. **Healthcare Expertise**: Validated our ability to handle safety-critical medical device guidance
4. **Regulatory Foundation**: Government win supports future Singapore HSA and ASEAN medical device submissions
5. **Market Entry Advantage**: Healthcare organizations and investors trust government-validated technology

### Why Healthcare Track is the Hardest
1. **Safety-Critical**: Medical device guidance has life/safety implications (highest accuracy required)
2. **Elderly Users**: 65+ with low digital literacy = hardest user demographic to serve
3. **Multilingual Medical Accuracy**: Medical terminology must be culturally appropriate across 5 languages
4. **Accessibility Standards**: WCAG 2.2 and medical accessibility compliance required
5. **Government Scrutiny**: Healthcare track judged by medical and technical experts

### How to Position This Win
**Framework**: "We proved our technology with the hardest problem (medical devices for elderly), now we're applying it to $50B commercial markets (enterprise/consumer) that generate revenue today."

**Key Message**: Healthcare win = technology validation, not our go-to-market strategy

## INVESTOR Q&A PREP

### Q: "What was the competition exactly?"
**A**: "The Pan-SEA AI Developer Challenge is a regional competition organized by AI Singapore—Singapore's National Research Foundation initiative to drive AI innovation in Southeast Asia. It had four tracks: Healthcare, Education, Finance, and Public Sector. We won the healthcare track, which asked: How can AI reimagine healthcare access in Southeast Asia? We answered by building a platform that teaches elderly users with low digital literacy to safely use medical devices."

### Q: "How many teams competed?"
**A**: "AI Singapore hasn't publicly released exact participant numbers, but the competition structure—cloud credits, mentorship, prize pool—indicates a highly competitive regional field. What matters: government judges evaluated our solution as the best healthcare application of AI Singapore's SEA-LION LLM for real-world Southeast Asian healthcare challenges."

### Q: "Why enterprise if you won healthcare?"
**A**: "Strategic capital efficiency. Healthcare validated our technology works for the hardest use case—safety-critical guidance for elderly users. But medical device software requires 12-18 months of regulatory approval. Enterprise and consumer generate revenue immediately with no regulatory barriers. We prove commercial traction first, then use that revenue to fund the medical regulatory pathway. This de-risks the business—we're not dependent on a single slow-moving regulatory approval."

### Q: "Does this give you regulatory advantages?"
**A**: "Yes, in three ways: (1) Government validation strengthens Singapore HSA medical device software submissions, (2) Demonstrates elderly accessibility compliance required for medical software, (3) Proves multilingual medical accuracy critical for ASEAN Medical Device Directive. We're also connected to AI Singapore mentors with healthcare regulatory expertise."
