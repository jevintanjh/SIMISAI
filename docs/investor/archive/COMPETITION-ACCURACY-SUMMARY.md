## THE ACCURATE STATEMENT

### Official Competition
**Pan-SEA AI Developer Challenge 2025**
- Organizer: AI Singapore (Singapore Government National Research Foundation initiative)
- Four tracks: Healthcare, Education, Finance, Public Sector
- SIMISAI won: **Healthcare Track - 1st Place**

### Healthcare Track Problem Statement
**"Reimagining Healthcare Access and Intelligence in Southeast Asia using LLM-related solutions"**

### SIMISAI's Solution
Universal visual guidance platform teaching elderly users (65+) with low digital literacy to safely use medical devices through:
- Computer vision (YOLOv8 + MediaPipe): 95%+ device detection accuracy
- AR overlays for step-by-step guidance
- AI Singapore's SEA-LION LLM for multilingual chat (5 Southeast Asian languages)
- Results: 85%+ task completion rate with elderly users

## KEY FACTS

### About SEA Lion
- **SEA Lion** = **S**outheast **A**sian **L**anguages **i**n **O**ne **N**etwork
- Open-source LLM family developed by AI Singapore
- Trained on 11 Southeast Asian languages
- Purpose-built for regional cultures, contexts, technical/medical terminology
- SIMISAI uses SEA-LION for multilingual AI chat guidance

### About AI Singapore
- National AI program by Singapore's National Research Foundation (NRF)
- Mission: Position Singapore as global AI leader
- Focus: Deep national AI capabilities, social and economic impact
- Structure: Collaboration between research institutions, startups, corporations
- Resources: Mentorship, cloud credits, funding programs (I2Start)

### About the Healthcare Track
- Problem: Southeast Asian healthcare accessibility crisis
- Focus: Elderly users, multilingual guidance, medical device safety
- Challenge: Low digital literacy, 680M population, 11+ languages
- Judging: Innovation, healthcare impact, feasibility, scalability, team capability
- Winner criteria: Best application of SEA-LION LLM for real-world healthcare problem
