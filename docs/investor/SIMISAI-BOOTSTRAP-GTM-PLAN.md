## What to Skip (For Now)

### Don't Spend Money On:

❌ **Industry Association Memberships** ($500-2K)
   - Wait until you have 1-2 pilots signed
   - ROI unclear at POC stage

❌ **Paid Conferences** ($1-2K per event)
   - Apply for speaking slots (free)
   - Attend networking sessions only (often free)
   - Wait until you have case studies

❌ **Paid Advertising** (LinkedIn, Google Ads)
   - B2B sales are relationship-driven
   - Ads don't work for pilot outreach

❌ **PR Agencies** ($5-10K/month)
   - DIY press releases (free distribution)
   - LinkedIn posts reach same audience

❌ **Booth at Trade Shows** ($10-30K)
   - Visitor badges are cheap ($100-300)
   - Network without exhibiting

❌ **Professional Copywriters/Designers** ($2-5K)
   - Use free tools (Canva, ChatGPT for copy)
   - Authenticity > polish at POC stage
