## 🎯 QUICK WORKFLOW

### Step-by-Step Process (15 minutes per image):

1. **Click direct search URL** for high-priority image
2. **Browse first 20 results** quickly
3. **Select top 2-3 candidates** that meet criteria
4. **Click candidate image** to view full size
5. **Check resolution**: Must be 1920x1080 minimum (look for "Large" or "Original" download option)
6. **Verify licensing**: Unsplash/Pexels are free for commercial use, double-check if unsure
7. **Download highest resolution** available
8. **Document selection** using template above (photo URL, photographer, why it fits)
9. **Save to organized folder**: `/images/slide-01/`, `/images/slide-02/`, etc.

**Estimated Time**:
- 6 high-priority images × 15 min each = 90 minutes
- Can be done in 2-3 sessions: Slides 1-2 (30 min), Slides 3-7 (30 min), Slide 10 (15 min)

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR FASTER SOURCING

### Search Strategy:

1. **Start broad, then narrow**:
   - Try "elderly asian healthcare" first
   - Then narrow to "elderly filipino woman blood pressure"

2. **Use photographer profiles**:
   - If you find one good image, check photographer's other work
   - They often have similar themed photos

3. **Filter by Orientation**:
   - Most slides need "Landscape" orientation
   - Exception: Portrait shots for testimonials (Slide 7)

4. **Check Collections**:
   - Unsplash: Look for curated collections on "Healthcare", "Elderly", "Asian"
   - Pexels: Use "Explore" to find related photo sets

5. **Quality over perfect ethnicity**:
   - If perfect Southeast Asian photo not available, prioritize:
     1. Emotional authenticity
     2. Medical device visible/clear
     3. High image quality
     4. East Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) as close alternative

### Common Challenges:

**Challenge**: "Not enough Southeast Asian subjects"
- **Solution**: Use "East Asian" as alternative, or search by specific country: "Filipino", "Indonesian", "Thai", "Vietnamese"

**Challenge**: "Images feel too clinical/staged"
- **Solution**: Add "home", "natural light", "authentic" to search terms

**Challenge**: "Medical devices not visible"
- **Solution**: Search specific device names: "glucose meter", "blood pressure cuff", "pulse oximeter"

**Challenge**: "Can't find multi-generational families"
- **Solution**: Search "grandparent grandchild caring" or use single elderly person as alternative

### Image Specifications:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| Minimum Resolution | 1920x1080 (Full HD) |
| Preferred Resolution | 3000x2000 or higher |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| File Size | 500KB - 5MB (optimal) |
| Color Space | sRGB |
| Orientation | Landscape (except Slide 7 testimonial: portrait OK) |

### Licensing Requirements:

✅ **Approved Sources**:
- Unsplash: Free for commercial use, no attribution required (but appreciated)
- Pexels: Free for commercial use, no attribution required
- Pixabay: Free for commercial use under Pixabay License

❌ **Avoid**:
- Getty Images (expensive, watermarked in free version)
- Shutterstock (requires paid license)
- Google Images (mixed licensing, risky)
- AI-generated images (per user preference)

## 📞 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Issue: "Can't find any good Southeast Asian elderly photos"
**Solution**:
1. Try East Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) as alternative
2. Search by specific nationality: "Filipino elderly", "Indonesian grandmother"
3. Prioritize emotional authenticity over perfect ethnicity match

### Issue: "Medical devices not visible in photos"
**Solution**:
1. Search specific device names instead of generic "medical device"
2. Accept photos where device is partially visible (context is enough)
3. Consider using diagram/illustration for device, photo for person separately

### Issue: "All images feel too stock-photo-ish"
**Solution**:
1. Add "candid", "authentic", "documentary" to search terms
2. Browse photographers who specialize in healthcare documentary work
3. Look for images with natural lighting and real-world settings

### Issue: "Images too low resolution"
**Solution**:
1. Always click "Download" button and select "Large" or "Original" size
2. Never use the preview/thumbnail image
3. Check dimensions after download (right-click > Properties on Windows, Get Info on Mac)
