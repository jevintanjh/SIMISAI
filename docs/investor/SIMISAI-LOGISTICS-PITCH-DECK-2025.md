## Pitch Deck Design Guidelines

### Visual Style:
- **Clean, modern, minimalist** (think Apple/Stripe aesthetics)
- **Color palette**:
  - Primary: Deep blue (#0F4C81)
  - Accent: Green (#10B981) for checkmarks/success
  - Alert: Red (#EF4444) for problems/errors
  - Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Typography**:
  - Headlines: Sans-serif bold (Helvetica Neue, Inter)
  - Body: Sans-serif regular
  - Data: Monospace for numbers (JetBrains Mono)
- **Icons**: Use emojis for quick visual recognition

### Slide Layout:
- **Title**: Top-left, large and bold
- **Content**: Left-aligned, plenty of white space
- **Data**: Tables, charts, or bullet points
- **Footer**: Company logo, slide number, sources (small text)

### Images to Include:
1. **Slide 2**: Chart showing 15-20% labor shortage vs 5% national average

## Pitch Delivery Tips

### Opening (2 minutes):
- Start with the crisis: "15-20% labor shortage, 35-40% volume growth, 3-5% margins"
- Hook: "80% of logistics companies can't afford traditional automation"
- Credibility: "We won AI Singapore's Healthcare Track"

### Middle (10-12 minutes):
- Problem → Solution → Demo → Moat → ROI
- Use the "Rule of 3": Three crises, three advantages, three steps
- Data-driven: Use specific numbers, cite sources
- Visual: Demo video is critical (show don't tell)

### Close (2-3 minutes):
- Recap: "Healthcare-grade accuracy, multilingual AI, 6-12 month payback"
- Urgency: "Labor shortage worsens every month, volume grows 35-40% annually"
- Clear ask: "30-minute discovery call, 60-day pilot, 2-3 weeks to launch"

### Q&A Preparation:
**Common Questions**:
1. "How do you compare to [Competitor]?" → Slide 7 (Multilingual Advantage)
2. "What's your proof this works?" → Slide 8 (Healthcare Credibility) + Pilot results
3. "How much does it really cost?" → Slide 9 (ROI Comparison) + Government grants
4. "How long to deploy?" → Slide 10 (2-4 weeks) + Pilot timeline
5. "What if it doesn't work?" → Pilot success criteria, subsidized pricing, refundable

## Deck Variants

### **15-Minute Version (Full Deck)**:
- All 11 slides
- 1-2 minutes per slide
- Include demo video (30 seconds)
- Q&A: 5 minutes

### **10-Minute Version (Abbreviated)**:
- Slides 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 (7 slides)
- Skip workflow details (Slide 4)
- Skip demo screenshots (Slide 6, just mention video)
- Skip pilot structure (Slide 10, just mention 60 days)

### **5-Minute Version (Elevator Pitch)**:
- Slides 1, 2, 5, 9, 11 (5 slides)
- Focus on crisis + solution + ROI + call to action
- No demo video, just screenshots
- Save deep-dive for follow-up
