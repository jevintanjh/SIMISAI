# SIMISAI Post-Pitch Follow-Up Sequence
## Investor Communication Playbook (Sabri Suby Style)

**Created**: November 2025
**Purpose**: Systematic investor follow-up after pitch presentations
**Framework**: Sabri Suby's direct, value-first communication principles

---

## 📋 Overview

After pitching SIMISAI to investors, systematic follow-up is critical. This document provides email templates, timing, and strategy for converting investor interest into commitments.

### Key Principles

1. **Speed**: Follow up within 1 hour while you're fresh in their mind
2. **Value First**: Every email provides value, not just "checking in"
3. **Direct Communication**: Sabri Suby style—no fluff, clear CTA
4. **Persistence**: 5-7 touchpoints before giving up
5. **Tracking**: Note investor interest level after each interaction

---

## 🎯 Follow-Up Timeline

| Timeframe | Purpose | Action | Materials |
|-----------|---------|--------|-----------|
| **1 Hour** | Thank you + recap | Email with key docs | Pitch deck PDF, one-pager |
| **24 Hours** | Value add | Share relevant news/data | Competition coverage, pilot interest |
| **3-5 Days** | Address objections | Preemptive FAQ | Financial model, due diligence deck |
| **1 Week** | Create urgency | Update on funding progress | Committed capital update |
| **2 Weeks** | Final push | Direct ask | Calendar link for deep dive |
| **Monthly** | Stay warm | Progress updates | Milestone achievements |

- Position of strength: revenue, users, government validation

Attached is our detailed regulatory strategy document showing:
- Singapore HSA timeline (if we pursue medical)
- Competitive landscape (who's stuck in regulatory hell)

[YOUR NAME]

## 📊 INVESTOR TRACKING SYSTEM

After each interaction, categorize investors:

### **Hot 🔥** (Likely to Invest)
- Characteristics: Asked detailed due diligence questions, requested term sheet, scheduled follow-up calls
- Action: Daily/every-other-day communication, provide all requested materials immediately
- Goal: Close within 7-14 days

### **Warm 🌡️** (Interested but Hesitant)
- Characteristics: Engaged with pitch, opened follow-up emails, but hasn't committed
- Action: Weekly follow-ups with value-adds (news, customer wins, objection handling)
- Goal: Convert to Hot or identify blockers

### **Cold ❄️** (Low Interest)
- Characteristics: No response to 3+ follow-ups, declined meetings, ghosting
- Action: Final "in or out" email, then move to monthly updates
- Goal: Archive or move to future Series A list

### **Out 🚫** (Passed Explicitly)
- Characteristics: Said "no" directly, cited specific deal-breaker
- Action: Thank them, ask for referrals to other investors, move to quarterly updates
- Goal: Learn from objection, stay warm for Series A

## 🎯 RESPONSE HANDLING SCRIPTS

```
Great! What specifically would be most helpful?

Here's what I have ready:
- Full financial model (3-year projections)
- Customer pilot agreements (redacted)
- Technical validation report (AI Singapore)
- Competitive analysis deep dive
- Cap table and use of funds breakdown

Let me know which of these you'd like, or if you need something else.

### If They Reply: "Not the right fit for us"

**Response**:
```
No problem—I appreciate you being direct.

Quick question: Is it the stage (too early), sector (not investing in AI/healthcare), or something about SIMISAI specifically?

Just trying to learn so I can improve the pitch.

Also, do you know any investors who DO focus on [stage/sector/geography] that I should connect with?

Thanks again for your time.
```

### If They Reply: "Circle back in 3 months"

**Response**:
```
Understood. Just so you know, our seed round closes March 31st, so the next opportunity will be Series A (likely Q3 2025, 3-5x higher valuation).

If that works for your timeline, I'll add you to our Series A early access list.

In the meantime, I'll send monthly progress updates so you can track our traction.

Sound good?
```

**After Follow-Up #5 (2 weeks)**, send:

I've sent 5 follow-ups and haven't heard back, so I'm assuming SIMISAI isn't the right fit right now.

No hard feelings—I know you're busy and see hundreds of pitches.

I'll stop reaching out, but feel free to reach back if your thesis changes.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]

P.S. - I'll send quarterly updates on major milestones. If you ever want back in the loop, just reply "add me back."
```

## 🚀 SUCCESS METRICS

Track these metrics to optimize your follow-up:

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Response Rate** | 50%+ reply to Follow-Up #1 | Responses / Pitches |
| **Meeting Conversion** | 30%+ schedule deep dive | Meetings / Responses |
| **Term Sheet Rate** | 20%+ request term sheet | Term Sheets / Meetings |
| **Close Rate** | 10%+ commit capital | Commitments / Pitches |
| **Time to Close** | 14-21 days average | Days from pitch to wire |

**If Below Target**:
- Response rate low? → Improve Follow-Up #1 subject line and personalization
- Meeting conversion low? → Add more value in Follow-Up #2-3
- Term sheet rate low? → Address objections earlier in sequence
- Close rate low? → Increase urgency and scarcity
- Time to close slow? → Shorten follow-up intervals

### 1. **Lead with Value, Not Neediness**
❌ "Just checking in"

❌ "Our round is open for a few months"

❌ "We still have room in the round"

## 🎯 FINAL TIPS

1. **Track Everything**: Use a CRM or spreadsheet to track every interaction
2. **Personalize Every Email**: Reference specific questions, concerns, or comments from their pitch
3. **Test Subject Lines**: A/B test different subject lines to see what gets opened
4. **Be Persistent, Not Annoying**: 5-6 strategic follow-ups over 2 weeks, then monthly
5. **Always Provide Value**: News, data, customer wins—never "just checking in"
6. **Create Real Urgency**: Use actual deadlines (round close, pilot launch, government grants)
7. **Ask for Referrals**: If they pass, always ask who else they'd recommend
