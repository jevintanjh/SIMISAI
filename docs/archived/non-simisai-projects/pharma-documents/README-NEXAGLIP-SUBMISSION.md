## BIOWAIVER VS BE STUDY ANALYSIS

### Sitagliptin BCS Classification
**BCS Class:** III (High Solubility, Low Permeability)
**Biowaiver Eligibility:** YES (per WHO/FDA/EMA guidelines)

### Biowaiver Path (RECOMMENDED)

**Requirements:**
1. Rapid dissolution: >85% in 30 minutes in pH 1.2, 4.5, 6.8
2. f2 similarity factor: ≥50 in all 3 pH media
3. Similar excipients: Same type, similar amounts vs reference product

**Advantages:**
- Cost: $5-10K (dissolution testing) vs $100-300K (BE study)
- Time: 4-6 weeks vs 12 months
- No human subjects required

**Risks:**
- If dissolution data inadequate → Must conduct BE study (major delay)
- If excipients significantly different → May not qualify

**Next Step:** Review <711> Dissolution.pdf within 3 days to extract comparative data

### BE Study Path (Fallback)

**When Required:**
- f2 < 50 in any pH medium
- Dissolution not rapid (≤85% in 30 min)
- Excipients significantly different
- HSA specifically requests BE study

**Timeline:** 12 months
**Cost:** $100,000 - $300,000 USD
**Requirements:** 90% CI for AUC and Cmax within 80.00-125.00%
